Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Showrunner ("Showrunner", "we", "us") is a product of Core Commerce LLC. This policy explains what the Showrunner Chrome extension and the showrunner.tools website collect, why, and what we do with it. The short version: the extension keeps your data on your computer, and we only receive what you choose to send us.

1. The Chrome extension

What it reads

The extension reads your own live show's seller dashboard on whatnot.com while it is open: the current item's title, price, timer, bid count and, when an auction is won, the buyer's username. It needs this to print labels. It does not read other pages, your messages, your payment details or your account settings.

What it stores, on your computer only

  • Your settings: chosen printer, label size, label fields, seller line and switches.
  • A history of recent sales from your shows (item title, price, buyer username, time), so you can reprint labels.
  • The email you type in the feedback form, so you don't have to type it again.

This stays in Chrome's storage on your computer. We never see it unless you send it to us.

What it talks to

  • Your label printer, through the DYMO software or the Showrunner Print Bridge running on your own computer. Label contents go to your printer, not to us.
  • Showrunner, only when you send feedback with the ? button: your message, your email, the extension version and, if you leave the box ticked, your printer settings and your last few sales (item titles, prices, buyer usernames and times) to help us diagnose problems.

The extension never clicks, bids, posts or sends messages on whatnot.com, and it does not send your show data anywhere else.

2. The showrunner.tools website

  • Contact form: your email, topic and message.
  • Sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile photo from Google.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Signing in uses the storage Firebase needs to keep you signed in.

3. How we use it

To answer you, fix bugs, decide what to build next and, once accounts exist, to run your account and subscription. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it except with the service providers that run Showrunner (Google Cloud and Firebase, for hosting, sign-in and storage) or when the law requires it.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Buyers' usernames

Buyer usernames appear on labels and in your sale history because they are on your own seller dashboard. They stay on your computer unless you include recent sales in a feedback report, in which case we use them only to diagnose that report.

5. Keeping and deleting

Feedback is kept as long as it helps us support you and improve Showrunner. To have your feedback or account data deleted, email contact@core-commerce.io. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored on your computer.

6. Children

Showrunner is for sellers and is not intended for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the extension or by email for material changes.
